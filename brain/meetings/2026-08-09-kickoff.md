# Kickoff: THE COMPANY OS

**Date:** 2026-08-09

## What was decided

- Build a file-based `/brain` as source of truth, with a Next.js app as
  reader/index on top (see [[2026-08-09-file-based-brain]]).
- Company: Business & Fitness — a growth agency for marketing agencies.
  Backend offer: 40–60 qualified appointments/month. Frontend offer:
  $5k–$20k new monthly revenue. ICP: agencies doing $5k–$40k/month.
- Departments: Marketing, Sales, Human Resources, Operations, Finance —
  Operations explicitly covers customer success and fulfillment.
- No real team yet beyond Marko — sample people seeded for Customer
  Success, Fulfillment, and Outbound Setter roles to show the shape.
- Tools: use whatever's already connected and free/cheap for now
  (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Canva, Trello, Atlassian) —
  confirm real login owners and cost later.
- No existing playbooks or training content — draft v1 versions seeded
  for cold email, cold SMS, and the appointment-setting call script;
  training is placeholder structure only.
- Everything sample or first-draft is explicitly marked as such in the
  file itself.

## Next step

Marko reviews Phase 1 (`/brain` folder only, no app yet), corrects
anything wrong — especially the company name assumption in
[[company]] — then Phase 2 (the Next.js reader app) starts.
