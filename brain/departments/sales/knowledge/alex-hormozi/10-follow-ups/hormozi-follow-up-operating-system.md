Module H10 of the [[alex-hormozi]] curriculum — Follow-Ups.

> Source: *$100M Leads* and the *$100M Playbook — Lead Nurture* section,
> Alex Hormozi (page references inline below), plus workshop notes.
> Reproduced here for internal training use only.

## Overview

You don't need "all my files." You need one ruthless follow-up system you
actually run every day. This is that system — one operating model that
covers the live call, a booked follow-up, an unbooked follow-up, and
long-term "they ghosted / not now" leads. Built for B2C coaching offers
(originally scoped to a menopause coaching offer, but applies to any
one-call-close B2C sale).

## 1. The rule on every call: BAMFAM

Never end a live call without one of two outcomes:

1. They buy.
2. You **B**ook **A** **M**eeting **F**rom **A** **M**eeting (BAMFAM) — a
   date and time on the calendar before you hang up.

Scheduling the next appointment while you're still on the call, in real
time, massively increases second-call show rates and sales. *(\$100M
Playbook — Lead Nurture, p.37)*

**Script for the end of the call, if not closing now:**

> "Sounds like you just need a bit of space to think. Totally fine. Let's
> do this so it doesn't drag on for weeks: let's put a 15-minute decision
> call on the calendar for [DAY/TIME]. If it's a yes, great, we start. If
> it's a no, no hard feelings, we part as friends. Does [DAY/TIME] work?"

No "I'll think about it and get back to you." That's you choosing
follow-up hell.

## 2. If a follow-up call IS booked

### Right after the call (within 2 hours)

Email + SMS:

- Summary of their goal and pain, in their own words
- The plan you recommend
- Confirm the next call time + link
- Clear expectation: "Next call is a yes/no, not more information."

**Example SMS:**

> "Hey [NAME], great talking today. Quick recap: you want to [GOAL] and
> you're sick of [PAIN]. On [DAY/TIME] we'll decide yes/no on the 6-month
> plan we outlined so you're not stuck in the same spot next year. Deal?"

### Reminder cadence to the follow-up

- **24 hours before:** automated reminder
- **Morning of:** manual text from a real phone
- **1 hour before:** manual text + calendar reminder

This "stacked" reminder pattern is what pushes show rates toward the
ceiling. *(\$100M Playbook — Lead Nurture, p.37, plus workshop notes)*

## 3. If they did NOT book a follow-up (or say "I'll think about it")

Treat the next 7 days as the "hot window." Money is made here.

**Day 0 (within 2 hours) — Recap + clear next step**

Email + SMS:

- 3 bullets: their goal, their pain, your plan
- Direct CTA: link to enroll, or link to book a decision call

**Day 2 — Objection answer.** Hit the main objection they gave:

> "Most women who say 'I need to think' are really worried about
> [TIME/MONEY/FAILURE]. Here's how we handle that inside the program…"

CTA: "Reply back with your main hesitation or grab a spot here: [LINK]."

**Day 4 — Proof.** Short case study of someone just like them and what
happened in 12–24 weeks. CTA: enroll / call.

**Day 7 — Soft break-up**

> "Hey [NAME], haven't heard back, which usually means timing isn't
> right. I'll go ahead and close your file so I'm not bugging you. If you
> want me to keep a spot open for you this month, reply 'HOLD' and I'll
> send you the next steps."

People often respond here because you're taking the offer away.

## 4. Long-term nurture (for all non-buyers & ghosters)

After Day 7, they move into value + pulse:

- Weekly or bi-weekly value emails (real tips they can use)
- Every 30–60 days: a reactivation prompt using a 9-word-style message:

> "Are you still looking to [MAIN OUTCOME THEY WANTED]?" *(\$100M Leads,
> p.66)*

No images, no links. Just the question. This is stupidly effective at
waking up old leads. *(\$100M Leads, p.66)* Ten ready-to-use variants of
this line, templated per offer type, are in
[[hormozi-9-word-reactivation-examples]].

You can also run a simple 3/7/30 sequence for "dead" leads *(workshop
notes)*:

- **Day 3:** Story / proof
- **Day 7:** FAQ / objection killer
- **Day 30:** value asset (e.g. "cheat sheet" relevant to the offer) +
  strong CTA

## 5. Daily execution OS

Simple, not sexy:

1. Every lead in a sheet/CRM must always have a **Next Touch Date**.
2. You or a setter spend the first 60–90 minutes daily doing follow-ups
   in this order:
   - Today's hot 0–7 day leads
   - Show-ups who didn't buy in the last 30 days
   - Older list (send value + 9-word reactivations)
3. Never end a call or DM without either:
   - Money collected, or
   - Next call booked.

## Key Takeaways

- BAMFAM: every live call ends with a sale or a booked next call — no
  "I'll think about it."
- A booked follow-up gets a same-day recap + a stacked reminder cadence
  (24h / morning-of / 1h before).
- An unbooked follow-up gets a structured 7-day hot-window sequence:
  recap → objection → proof → soft break-up.
- Past Day 7, leads go into long-term nurture: periodic value content
  plus a plain 9-word reactivation question, no links or images.
- The whole system runs on one non-negotiable habit: every lead has a
  Next Touch Date, worked in priority order, every single day.
