Module H10 of the [[alex-hormozi]] curriculum — Follow-Ups. Ten
ready-to-use variants of the 9-word reactivation line referenced in
[[hormozi-follow-up-operating-system]].

> Source: *$100M Leads*, Alex Hormozi, p.66 (the 9-word email concept),
> adapted into 10 ready-to-use variants for gyms, coaching, agency, and
> SaaS offers. Reproduced here for internal training use only.

## Overview

Same underlying idea as the original 9-word email — but templated for
money, leads, and calls specifically, across the offer types we actually
run. Use these as SMS, WhatsApp, or plain-text email — never with images
or links in the first message.

## The 10 variants

**1. Clients / customers**

> "Are you still looking to add 20 new clients this month?"

Swap: "20" → the specific number they said. "clients" → members,
customers, subscribers. "this month" → this quarter / before summer / Q4.

**2. Calendar full of calls**

> "Are you still trying to get your calendar full of sales calls?"

Swap: "calendar full of sales calls" → 10–15 quality demos a week, daily
consults, booked intro sessions.

**3. Revenue target**

> "Are you still aiming to hit 50,000 in monthly revenue?"

Swap: "50,000" → their MRR / monthly sales target. "monthly revenue" →
monthly EFT, retainer revenue, ad spend managed, etc.

**4. Capacity goal**

> "Are you still serious about filling your gym to 200 members?"

Swap: "gym" → agency, program, studio, practice. "200 members" → specific
capacity goal (100 clients, 15 retainers, 40 seats).

**5. Cost / efficiency**

> "Are you still looking for a way to lower your ad costs?"

Swap: "lower your ad costs" → raise close rate, increase show rate,
improve retention, increase profit per client.

**6. Income replacement**

> "Are you still planning to replace your 9–5 income this year?"

Swap: "replace your 9–5 income" → quit your job, open a second location,
double your take-home. "this year" → before year-end, in the next 6
months.

**7. Lead volume**

> "Are you still open to getting 20–30 qualified leads every month?"

Swap: "20–30" → a number that feels big but believable. "qualified
leads" → booked intros, strategy sessions, discovery calls.

**8. MRR goal**

> "Are you still interested in turning your group program into 20k MRR?"

Swap: "group program" → online course, membership, agency, SaaS. "20k
MRR" → their stated recurring number.

**9. Pain reversal**

> "Are you still tired of chasing leads instead of taking booked calls?"

Swap: "chasing leads" → cold DM'ing, discounting, doing free work.
"taking booked calls" → running scheduled demos, closing from your
calendar.

**10. System / process**

> "Are you still wanting a simple system to close more of your calls?"

Swap: "simple system" → step-by-step script, repeatable process,
done-with-you setup. "close more of your calls" → raise your close rate,
convert more intros, sign more retainers.

## How to actually use these

- One line. No links or images in the first message. *($100M Leads,
  p.66)*
- Send to: no-shows, "let me think about it," lost deals, old leads.
- When they reply "yes / kind of / maybe," the only goal of your next
  message is to book the call:

  > "Cool, let's do this properly. I've got [TIME OPTIONS] for a quick
  > decision call. What works?"

## Key Takeaways

- These are all the same 9-word reactivation mechanic — just re-worded
  for the specific offer (clients, calls, revenue, capacity, cost,
  income, leads, MRR, pain, or process).
- Plain text only — no images, no links, on the first send.
- The reply isn't the win — the booked call is. Any positive reply gets
  routed straight into a time-options close, not a longer conversation.
