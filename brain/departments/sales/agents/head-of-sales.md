# Head of Sales

**Role type:** lead
**Department:** Sales
**Runtime:** Claude (`claude-opus-5`)
**Status:** active — [open the chat](/agents/head-of-sales/chat)

**Grounded in:** every `doc`-type file with `department: sales`
([[imperium-acquisition]], [[alex-hormozi]], and anything else filed under
Sales), plus its own
department's live Issues, Projects, and Scorecard KPIs — appointments
booked, show-up rate, close rate.

**What it's for:** a real pipeline pulse check — current numbers against
target, not just training content — and turning a follow-up or a fix into
tracked work.

**Real tools (scoped to the Sales department only):**
- read open Issues and Projects, and the latest Scorecard numbers
- create Issues and Projects
- move an Issue or Project to a new status
- log a real Scorecard entry against a defined KPI (e.g. this week's
  close rate)

It always tells you plainly what it did after using a tool — nothing
happens silently.
