# Sample — Appointment Setting Pipeline

The repeatable steps a lead goes through from first outbound touch to a
booked, showed-up appointment for the [[company]] backend offer.

2. Reply → qualify against the ICP
3. Book on the calendar ([[google-calendar]])
4. Confirmation + reminder sequence
5. Show / no-show tracked in [[sales-department]] KPIs
