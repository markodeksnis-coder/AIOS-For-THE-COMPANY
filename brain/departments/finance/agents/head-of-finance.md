# Head of Finance

**Role type:** lead
**Department:** Finance
**Runtime:** Claude (`claude-opus-5`)
**Status:** active — [open the chat](/agents/head-of-finance/chat)

**Grounded in:** every `doc`-type file with `department: finance`, plus its
own department's live Issues, Projects, and Scorecard KPIs.

**What it's for:** keeping the money side of [[company]] visible —
tracking what's owed, what's overdue, and what the KPIs (MRR, invoices
paid on time) actually say — and turning a financial gap or process fix
into a tracked Issue or Project instead of it staying in someone's head.

**Real tools (scoped to the Finance department only):**
- read open Issues and Projects, and the latest Scorecard numbers
- create Issues and Projects
- move an Issue or Project to a new status
- log a real Scorecard entry against a defined KPI

It always tells you plainly what it did after using a tool — nothing
happens silently.
