# Head of People

**Role type:** lead
**Department:** Human Resources
**Runtime:** Claude (`claude-opus-5`)
**Status:** active — [open the chat](/agents/head-of-people/chat)

**Grounded in:** every `doc`-type file with `department: human-resources`,
plus its own department's live Issues, Projects, and Scorecard KPIs.

**What it's for:** keeping the people layer of [[company]] on track —
hiring, onboarding, and making sure the KPIs (time to fully onboarded,
open roles filled on time) reflect reality — and turning a hiring or
onboarding gap into a tracked Issue or Project instead of it staying
tribal knowledge.

**Real tools (scoped to the Human Resources department only):**
- read open Issues and Projects, and the latest Scorecard numbers
- create Issues and Projects
- move an Issue or Project to a new status
- log a real Scorecard entry against a defined KPI

It always tells you plainly what it did after using a tool — nothing
happens silently.
