> **DRAFT v1.** First pass, correct freely.

## Goal

Same ICP as [[cold-email-follow-up]], different channel — used when a
lead has engaged elsewhere (opened multiple emails, visited a link) but
hasn't replied.

## Sequence (draft)

1. **Message 1.** Identify yourself and the company by name immediately
   — cold SMS with no context reads as spam and gets blocked.
2. **Message 2 (if no reply in 2 days).** One direct question tied to the
   problem, not a repeat of message 1.
3. **Stop after message 2** unless the lead replies — SMS has a much
   lower tolerance for repeated unsolicited contact than email.

## Rules

- Never SMS a lead who hasn't already been reached by email or another
  channel first.
- Keep every message under 2–3 sentences.
- Respect opt-outs immediately and permanently.
