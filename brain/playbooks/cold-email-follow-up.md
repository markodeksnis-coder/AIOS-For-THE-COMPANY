## Goal

Get a reply from a marketing agency owner in the ICP (see [[company]])
that moves the conversation toward a booked call.

## Sequence (draft)

1. **Day 0 — Initial email.** One clear line on the specific problem
   (inconsistent qualified appointments), one proof point, one low-friction ask.
2. **Day 3 — Bump.** Short, no new pitch — just "did this get buried?"
   energy, one line.
3. **Day 7 — New angle.** Reframe around the backend offer promise (40–60
   qualified appointments/month) instead of repeating the first email.
4. **Day 14 — Breakup.** Polite close-the-loop message; leave the door
   open, stop the sequence either way.

## Rules

- Stop the sequence immediately on any reply, positive or negative.
- Never send more than one email per day to the same lead across all
  sequences.
- Every email should be answerable in one line — if it needs a paragraph
  to reply to, it's too much to ask.
