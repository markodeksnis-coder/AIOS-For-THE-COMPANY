> **DRAFT v1.** First pass, correct freely.

## Goal

Turn a warm reply or inbound interest into a booked, qualified
appointment — "qualified" meaning the lead genuinely fits the ICP in
[[company]] (agency doing $5k–$40k/month) and has a real reason to want
more appointments now.

## Structure (draft)

1. **Open.** Confirm timing, state the reason for the call in one
   sentence.
2. **Qualify.** Confirm revenue range, current outbound situation, and
   whether appointment volume is actually the bottleneck.
3. **Position.** Tie the backend offer promise (40–60 qualified
   appointments/month) to what they said their bottleneck is — don't
   pitch generically.
4. **Book.** Get a specific date/time on the calendar before ending the
   call, not a vague "I'll follow up."

## Disqualifiers (draft)

- Revenue clearly outside the $5k–$40k/month range
- No real budget or decision-making authority on the call
